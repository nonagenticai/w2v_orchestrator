# Contact Page Demo Patterns

When recording a contact page:

1. **Contact info** — Navigate and show contact information (address, phone, email)
2. **Form fields** — Highlight the contact form fields
3. **Form interaction** — Demonstrate form interaction using `slowType` for name, email, and message
4. **Map embed** — Show the map embed if present, scroll to it
5. **Social links** — Annotate social media links or alternative contact methods
6. **Submit button** — Annotate the submit button but do NOT click it

Use `slowType` for form demonstration to show the form in action. Do NOT submit the form — only annotate the submit button.
