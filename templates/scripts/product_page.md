# Product Page Demo Patterns

When recording a product page:

1. **Navigate** — Go to the product page
2. **Product hero** — Highlight the product image/title
3. **Description** — Scroll to and highlight the description
4. **Pricing** — Highlight price information
5. **Features/specs** — Scroll through feature list
6. **Reviews** — Show customer reviews if present
7. **Add to cart** — Highlight the CTA button
