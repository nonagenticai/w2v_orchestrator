# Product Listing Demo Patterns

When recording a product listing or catalog page:

1. **Catalog header** — Navigate and annotate the page header with product count
2. **Filters/sorting** — Demonstrate filter controls and sorting options
3. **Product cards** — Scroll through products, highlight 3-4 product cards
4. **Product detail** — Click one product to show the detail view
5. **Back to listing** — Navigate back to the listing page
6. **Pagination** — Show pagination or load-more functionality

Use `highlightElement` on the filter sidebar. Pace product browsing naturally — viewers should be able to read product names and prices.
