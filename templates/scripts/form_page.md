# Form Page Demo Patterns

When recording a form/signup page:

1. **Navigate** — Go to the form page, annotate its purpose
2. **Overview** — Highlight the form container
3. **Field walkthrough** — For each important field:
   - Highlight the field with `highlightElement`
   - Click into it
   - Use `slowType` to enter demo data
   - Pause briefly between fields
4. **Validation** — If visible, show validation states
5. **Submit area** — Highlight the submit button but DON'T click it
6. **Annotate** — Final annotation about the form purpose
