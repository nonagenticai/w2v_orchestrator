# Blog Index Demo Patterns

When recording a blog listing page:

1. **Page title** — Navigate and annotate the blog header or title
2. **Featured post** — Highlight the featured or pinned post if present
3. **Recent posts** — Scroll through recent posts, annotate 2-3 titles
4. **Categories/tags** — Show the categories or tags sidebar if present
5. **Pagination** — Demonstrate pagination or infinite scroll
6. **Read a post** — Click through to one featured post to show the transition

Use `smoothScroll` to browse through the post list naturally. Keep annotations brief — focus on showing the breadth of content.
