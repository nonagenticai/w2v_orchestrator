# About Page Demo Patterns

When recording an about page:

1. **Introduction** — Navigate and show the company or team introduction
2. **Mission statement** — Highlight the mission statement or value proposition
3. **Team section** — Scroll to the team section, highlight 2-3 team members
4. **History/timeline** — Show the company timeline or history if present
5. **Partnerships** — Highlight partnership logos or certifications
6. **CTA** — Annotate any call-to-action (join us, contact, careers)

Use `smoothScroll` to move through sections. Annotate team member cards briefly — name and role are enough.
